During this project we were tasked with writing a research proposal, demonstrating an understanding of research paradigms, applying knowledge and skills in relation to ethical principles in research. We conducted data collection, analysis, and communicated research findings effectively. During research we discovered that the current market of mobile health applications indicates that there is a lack of dedicated peer-to-peer mobile apps that allow gym members to conveniently plan, exchange, or connect using guest/buddy passes securely and in compliance with policies. We therefore built a fully functional prototype that fills this gap.
