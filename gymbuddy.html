GYM BUDDY APP

During this project we were tasked with writing a research proposal, demonstrating an understanding of research paradigms, applying knowledge and skills in relation to ethical principles in research. We conducted data collection, analysis, and communicated research findings effectively. During research we discovered that the current market of mobile health applications indicates that there is a lack of dedicated peer-to-peer mobile apps that allow gym members to conveniently plan, exchange, or connect using guest/buddy passes securely and in compliance with policies. We therefore built a fully functional prototype that fills this gap. Below is the landing page and more.

Screenshots of the app

Login and Register Page

Above is the Login page, where users that have an account can just login and proceed using the app, the page also has a "register" button so that users that do not have an account can seamlessly navigate to the Registration page. The registration page is for user that are new to the app.

PassHolder Page

The PassHolders account is for members of the gym that want to invite a buddy to the gym. The PassHolder can create sessions that their buddies can request to join. The PassHolder can also edit the sessions they had created as preferred.

Non-PassHolder Page

This page displays the sessions created by the PassHolder and allows the NonPassHolder to request to join a preferred session. This page provides the status of a request sent to join a session. Once the PassHolder approves, the status changes.
